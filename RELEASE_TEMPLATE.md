## ReSubstreamer v1.0.0 — Initial Official Release 🚀

Welcome to the first official release of **ReSubstreamer**, the open-source modernization of Substreamer for Android!

This release delivers the beloved, minimalist aesthetic of the original application, reinforced with a rewritten playback engine, seamless carousel gestures, and fine-grained bandwidth controls.

---

### ✨ Highlights & New Features

* **Dual Bitrate Profiles:** Independent maximum streaming bitrates for **Wi-Fi** (lossless/FLAC/320k) and **Cellular Data** (configurable low-bandwidth presets).
* **Now Playing Carousel:** Horizontal artwork swipe to effortlessly switch tracks with zero latency.
* **Unified Gesture Queue:** Fluid swipe-up gesture available anywhere across the player interface to instantly reveal and manage the upcoming track queue.
* **Synchronized Artwork & Controls Transitions:** Smooth layout scaling animations when expanding or collapsing the playback sheet.
* **Modernized Audio Pipeline:** Upgraded background media playback service ensuring uninterrupted playback through Android 14/15 battery saving modes.
* **Refined Database Cache:** Snappy browsing and caching for self-hosted libraries exceeding 50,000 songs.
